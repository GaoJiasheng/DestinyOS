## description（2563/4000）

Explore Chinese and Western traditions in one thoughtfully crafted app. DestinyOS brings together seven charting systems—BaZi, Zi Wei Dou Shu, I Ching (Mei Hua and Liu Yao), Qi Men Dun Jia, tarot, Western astrology, and Vedic astrology—plus numerology.

CHARTS YOU CAN EXAMINE
Your core charts and readings come from a deterministic offline engine and a versioned knowledge library. See the chart, explore its symbols, and read the reasoning behind each interpretation. Core readings are not invented by AI at runtime.

YOUR TRADITIONS, YOUR LANGUAGE
Switch between English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. Learn unfamiliar symbols through the built-in library of tarot cards, hexagrams, and introductory guides.

A SKY GROUNDED IN REAL STARS
Discover a starfield based on a real star catalog. Explore native chart graphics and daily readings in a dark, gold-accented interface inspired by the night sky.

MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
Shuffle, cut, select, and flip tarot cards with gestures. Shake to cast Liu Yao, or use the button alternative. Keep daily readings close with local reminders and home and lock-screen widgets. Notification permission is optional.

WORKS OFFLINE
Use local charts, core interpretations, and daily readings without an account or internet connection. Birth profiles and saved readings are encrypted on your device. Signing in and syncing, PDF export, share links, membership purchases, and optional AI follow-up require a connection.

OPTIONAL AI FOLLOW-UP
Ask the Master is a separate online feature, clearly labeled as AI-generated and equipped with a report option. It does not generate the offline core readings. AI answers can be mistaken; use them for reflection.

FREE CHARTS, OPTIONAL AD REMOVAL
All chart systems are free. DestinyOS Pro removes ads: US$2.99 per month with an auto-renewing subscription, or US$6.99 once for lifetime ad removal. Local App Store prices apply. Membership is shared with the website when you use the same account. Restore purchases in Me → Membership; manage or cancel a subscription in Apple's subscription settings. Monthly subscriptions renew unless canceled before the current period ends. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

FOR ADULTS, FOR REFLECTION
For users aged 18 and over. Content is for entertainment, cultural learning, and self-reflection, not medical, psychological, legal, financial, investment, or other professional advice. No reading guarantees an outcome.
