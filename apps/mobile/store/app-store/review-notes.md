## 可粘贴英文备注

DestinyOS is a native React Native app for adults aged 18 and over. It combines seven Chinese and Western charting systems: BaZi, Zi Wei Dou Shu, I Ching (Mei Hua and Liu Yao), Qi Men Dun Jia, tarot, Western astrology, and Vedic astrology, plus numerology. Core charts and interpretations run offline using deterministic calculations and a versioned knowledge library. They are not generated by an AI model at runtime.

To try the core experience, complete onboarding and enter an adult birth profile, or skip and add one in Me. No account is required for local charts and readings. Open Readings to select a system; charts can be zoomed and their symbols inspected. Open Today and its calendar for daily readings. In Me → Settings, select English, Simplified Chinese, or Traditional Chinese. Tarot supports native gestures for shuffling, cutting, selecting and flipping; Liu Yao supports shaking or an equivalent button. Local reminders and home/lock-screen widgets are optional. Notification permission is not required for local readings.

For guideline 4.3, the distinctive experience is the combination of seven Chinese and Western traditions plus numerology, an on-device charting and interpretation engine, inspectable chart reasoning, three languages, and a starfield drawn from a real star catalog. The app also provides native gestures, haptics, local notifications and WidgetKit integration. It is not a website wrapper. Knowledge updates contain signed text data, not new executable functionality.

The optional online Ask the Master feature is separately marked as AI-generated and includes a report control. It requires sign-in and a network connection. AI output may be inaccurate. Birth input is not sent to third-party advertising services. Core offline readings do not depend on an AI service.

The first release is free, with no advertising, in-app purchases or subscriptions. No purchase or advertising SDK is linked in this binary, and no tracking authorization is requested. The existing products are not included in this submission. Signed-in users can keep their full history and use every sharing template and report export.

Sign-in is needed for cloud sync, PDF export, share links and optional AI follow-up. Me → Settings includes in-app account deletion.

Content is for entertainment, cultural learning and self-reflection only. It is not medical, psychological, legal, financial, investment or other professional advice, and it makes no promises about outcomes. The app displays disclaimers during onboarding and in reports.
