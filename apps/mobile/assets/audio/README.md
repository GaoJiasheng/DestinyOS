Original deterministic synthesized ritual sounds, generated by scripts/ritual-audio.py.

MP3 is bundled for native playback; OGG is included as an alternate encoding.
Each sound is shorter than 1.5 seconds and normalized to -14 LUFS with -1.5 dBTP ceiling.
No recordings or third-party samples are used.
