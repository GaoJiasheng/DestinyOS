This is the App Store icon for "天机 / DestinyOS", a bilingual (Chinese/English) astrology and destiny app covering Chinese Bazi, Zi Wei Dou Shu, I Ching, Qimen, Western astrology, tarot and Vedic astrology. The name 天机 means "the secrets of heaven", and 天璣 (Tianji, Phecda) is also the third star of the Big Dipper.
ICON REQUIREMENTS: exactly 1024x1024 square, fully opaque, full-bleed background reaching every edge (iOS rounds the corners itself, so do NOT draw a rounded square, frame, border, device mockup or drop shadow around the icon). One bold central emblem inside the middle 75% of the canvas, with a strong clear silhouette that still reads at 60x60 pixels: few large shapes, high contrast, no hairline detail that disappears when small. Absolutely no text, letters, Chinese characters, numbers or watermarks.
STYLE: mystical, otherworldly and luxurious, "gilded celestial" (鎏金天象): deep night-blue to ink-black background (#05070F to #111628) with a soft nebula glow, luminous gold leaf (#D4AF6A, highlights #E8D3A3) as the main material, small accents of cinnabar red (#C8412B) or indigo violet (#6B5BD2) only where noted. Rich lighting: inner glow, rim light, a few sparkling stars. Painterly and refined, like a premium fantasy game emblem crossed with an antique engraved astrolabe. NOT cartoon, NOT flat clip-art, NOT a thin-line geometric logo, no plastic gloss, no 3D-render cheapness.
